# SPLAGen Membership Website Rules and Discussion Forum Etiquette

Welcome to the SPLAGen membership website. The membership website is provided by SPLAGen for the purpose of creating a member-to-member space for discussion, collaboration, resource sharing, and communication related to genetic counseling, genetics education, research, advocacy, and access to genetic services for Latin American communities.

As a condition of initial and continuing access to the membership website, SPLAGen requires all members to agree to and follow these rules. These rules apply to all members who participate in or use the membership website.

## SPLAGen Membership Website Rules

Members must adhere to the following rules:

1. SPLAGen has made the membership website available to members for educational, informational, professional, and collaborative purposes related to SPLAGen’s mission. Other uses are prohibited.
2. Members are required to represent their identity (contact information) accurately at all times when accessing or using the membership website.
3. SPLAGen provides the membership website as a service to members. SPLAGen is not providing medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice through the membership website. Members must rely on their own professional judgment and applicable laws, institutional policies, and professional standards.
4. Members are prohibited from posting commercial messages, advertisements, political campaigning, repeated self-promotion, or solicitations to sell goods or services. Relevant and free professional opportunities, educational events, research invitations, job postings, or resources may be shared when they align with SPLAGen’s mission and are presented transparently.
5. Information posted on the membership website should not be considered confidential. Members must not post information that identifies patients, clients, students, colleagues, institutions, or other individuals without appropriate permission. Members should use extreme care when posting information that could be copied, downloaded, reproduced, or shared outside the website.
6. Members are responsible for the information they post. By posting content on the membership website, members acknowledge that other members may view, use, or reference that information within the context of the website, unless otherwise restricted by SPLAGen policy.
7. Members may not use the membership website to share confidential business information, proprietary information, pricing arrangements, terms of service, or other information in a way that could violate applicable laws or regulations.
8. Discussion topics, responses, and shared resources are for educational and informational purposes only. They should not be relied upon as individualized medical advice, formal clinical guidance, or official practice guidelines.
9. Members should exercise common sense, courtesy, and professionalism in all messages and posts. Members may not use the membership website to transmit defamatory, obscene, threatening, harassing, discriminatory, or otherwise offensive communications. Personal attacks and derogatory comments related to race, ethnicity, nationality, language, religion, social class, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, professional background, or any other personal characteristic are prohibited.
10. Members may not post or distribute files, articles, images, tools, presentations, or other materials that are subject to copyright, trademark, or other proprietary rights unless they have permission from the rights holder or the material is clearly available for sharing.
11. Members must respect the security and integrity of the membership website. Members may not attempt to gain unauthorized access to private areas of the website, another member’s account, administrative tools, or restricted SPLAGen content.
12. Members may not use the membership website for any purpose that is illegal, harmful, or inconsistent with SPLAGen’s mission. This includes, but is not limited to, sharing malware, spreading knowingly false information, engaging in fraud, or encouraging unlawful activity.
13. The membership website may not be used in a manner that violates applicable laws, SPLAGen policies, or any other rules or procedures established by SPLAGen.
14. SPLAGen may deny, suspend, or terminate access to the membership website for members who do not agree to or comply with these rules.

## Discussion Forum Etiquette

1. Posts should focus on topics related to SPLAGen’s mission, genetic counseling, genetics and genomics education, research, advocacy, access to genetic services, and Spanish- or Portuguese-language genetic counseling resources.
2. Use a clear and concise subject line or post title so members can understand the topic and search for the discussion later.
3. Allow others to share different experiences, perspectives, and points of view. If you disagree with another member, do so respectfully. Do not attack, mock, or repeatedly target another member.
4. Avoid posting the same or similar information repeatedly. Posts should advance the conversation.
5. Do not send administrative requests, such as membership questions, account changes, or requests to be removed from communications, to the full discussion forum. Members should use the website tools or contact SPLAGen administrators for assistance.

## Disclaimer

SPLAGen reserves the right to deny access, suspend accounts, or remove members who provide false, misleading, or incomplete identity information.

The views expressed on the membership website are those of individual members. They do not necessarily represent the views of SPLAGen, its leadership, committees, volunteers, or partner organizations.

SPLAGen does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or usefulness of information posted by members. Unless specifically stated otherwise, SPLAGen does not endorse, approve, recommend, or certify any information, advice, guidance, product, service, organization, or resource mentioned on the membership website.

SPLAGen reserves the right to monitor activity on the membership website and to remove messages, materials, or content that do not comply with these rules or that are not consistent with the purpose and values of SPLAGen.

## Moderation and Access

SPLAGen reserves the right, at its discretion, to remove postings or other content that violate these rules or are otherwise inconsistent with the purpose of the membership website.

Members who violate these rules may receive a warning, have content removed, have posting privileges limited, or have access to the membership website suspended or terminated.

## Updates to These Rules

SPLAGen may modify or update these rules as needed. Continued use of the membership website after updates are made indicates agreement to follow the current version of the rules.

## Acceptance

By joining or using the SPLAGen membership website, members acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agree to follow the SPLAGen Membership Website Rules and Discussion Forum Etiquette.
